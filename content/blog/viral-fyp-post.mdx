## Enter: Podloop

Instead of a human assistant, I routed all of my guests through a single **Podloop** link. 

Here is what happened:
1. **Zero Email Ping-Pong:** The guest picked a time.
2. **Instant Asset Collection:** Podloop forced them to upload their bio and a 4K headshot *before* the booking confirmed.
3. **Automated Hounding:** Podloop's cron jobs automatically emailed them the virtual studio link 24 hours before the show.

I didn't send a single email. And my show-up rate was 100%.


## The Verdict

The creator economy is shifting rapidly. The creators who win won't be the ones working 80-hour weeks editing audio and sending reminder emails. The winners will be the ones who leverage AI like Google Veo for their visuals, and automation platforms like **Podloop** for their logistics.
